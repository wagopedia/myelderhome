Older-Adult Fall Deaths by State: What the CDC's 2023 Numbers Show
Flagship study by Moe Kahok

Study summary

  • Falls are the leading cause of injury for Americans age 65 and older.
  • 41,400 older adults died from falls in 2023 — 69.9 deaths per 100,000 people age 65+.
  • In 2024, fall deaths rose to 43,020 nationally — 70.3 per 100,000.
  • Wisconsin had the highest state rate in 2023 at 158.4 per 100,000, followed by Minnesota (132.6) and Maine (126.5).
  • Alabama had the lowest rate at 29.5 per 100,000, followed by New Jersey (34.6).
  • Risk rises sharply with age: the 2024 rate for people 85+ was about 338 per 100,000; men (74.8) died at higher rates than women (66.6).

Data: CDC National Center for Health Statistics — 2023 final mortality data (NCHS Data Brief No. 532, June 2025); 2024 national update (MMWR, September 24, 2026).
